TECH SHOULD FIT INTO LIFE

Make space for your whole day.

Digital wellbeing is about noticing how technology affects your time, attention, mood and routines — then making choices that support you.

DAY
😴SleepGive your mind time to rest.

TRY A FEW SMALL HABITS

Little changes, realistic routines.

🌙
Create a wind-down routine

Choose a time to put devices aside before sleep and keep your device away from your pillow if possible.

⏳
Take intentional breaks

Pause between tasks, stretch, look away from the screen and notice how you feel.

🔕
Quiet unnecessary notifications

Keep alerts that matter and reduce interruptions during study, meals or rest.

🎯
Choose your purpose

Before opening an app, ask what you want to do — then check whether you are still doing it.

🌿
A QUICK SELF-REFLECTION

How does your screen time make you feel?

There is no perfect number that suits everyone. Notice whether your online time supports or interrupts the things that matter to you.

Choose the option that feels closest today.
