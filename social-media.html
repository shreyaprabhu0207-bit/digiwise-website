SOCIAL MEDIA WITH PURPOSE

Connect more. Compare less. Think first.

Social platforms can help us learn, create and stay in touch. Thoughtful habits help keep those benefits in balance.

📣

Pause before posting

Would you be comfortable if a teacher, family member or future version of you saw it?

💛

Disagree with ideas without insulting, embarrassing or targeting a person.

🛡️

KINDNESS COUNTS ONLINE

Your words have an impact — even through a screen.

Before sending a comment, message or reply, imagine how it might feel to receive it. If something online makes you uncomfortable, you do not have to handle it alone. Save relevant information, use platform reporting tools and speak with a trusted adult.
