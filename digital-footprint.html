Your digital footprint

The information and traces connected to your online activity can create a picture of your interests, actions and identity. Some traces are shared intentionally; others may be collected by apps and websites.

WHAT COUNTS AS PERSONAL INFORMATION?

Small details can reveal a lot.

🪪Identity detailsFull name, date of birth, school ID
📍Location cluesLive location, home or school details
🔑Account accessPasswords, one-time codes, recovery keys
📸Photos & mediaImages that reveal people or places
📱Usage informationSearches, clicks and app activity
💳Financial detailsPayment information and account numbers

If something feels wrong

Do not respond to suspicious requests for money, passwords or private images. Stop interacting, use the platform's block/report tools and speak to a trusted adult. If a serious online incident occurs, seek help from an appropriate adult and official support channel.
