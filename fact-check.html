DON'T LET A VIRAL POST DO THE THINKING

Surprising does not always mean true.

Misleading content can spread quickly through screenshots, edited clips, old photos and messages that lack context.

📰

Missing context

A real image or quote is shared with an incorrect date, place or explanation.

🎭

Manipulated media

Images, audio or video may be edited or generated to create a false impression.

⚡

Urgent chain messages

Messages pressure people to forward claims before checking them.
