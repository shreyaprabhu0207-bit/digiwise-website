First things first

Artificial intelligence is technology that can perform tasks that usually need human-like abilities, such as recognising patterns, understanding language or making predictions.

AI IN EVERYDAY LIFE

You may already use AI without noticing.

🎧Music suggestions

Recommends songs based on listening patterns.

🗺️Maps

Estimates routes and travel times.

📷Photo tools

Recognises faces or improves images.

💬AI chat tools

Generate text and help explain ideas.

USE IT THOUGHTFULLY

AI is a helper, not an unquestionable authority.

AI systems can produce useful explanations, but they can also make mistakes, miss context or reflect bias in their training data. A fluent answer is not proof that it is correct.

  • Use AI to brainstorm or clarify a difficult idea.
  • Check important facts with reliable sources.
  • Never enter passwords, private details or sensitive information.
  • Follow your teacher's rules and acknowledge AI help when required.
  • Keep your own thinking, judgement and creativity involved.
