WHY DIGIWISE EXISTS

Digital confidence is more than knowing which button to press.

Students meet AI-generated answers, short videos, group chats, online games and social platforms in everyday life. Knowing how to use a tool is only one part of digital literacy. It is also important to question information, protect personal details, treat people respectfully and recognise when it is time to take a break.

DIGIWISE brings these connected topics together in one simple educational website.

What this project is — and what it is not

DIGIWISE is a student-created educational website prototype developed as a digital awareness initiative. The current project uses desk research and publicly available guidance. It does not claim that a school visit, student survey, in-person awareness session or measured impact evaluation has taken place.
